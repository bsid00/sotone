Sotone needs a speech model

Sotone ships without one — no model weights are in the download or the installer.                           Sotone
                          
                          
                          
                            Sotone voce — spoken quietly and aside. Hold
                            a key, say one finding, release: it is transcribed
                            here and appended to a markdown note as one
                            timestamped line.

                            window you are testing.
